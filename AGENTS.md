# Repository research-update instructions

Before changing public research artifacts, follow the canonical [shared update rules](research/update-rules.html).

For each substantive completed research or publication milestone, update `research/timeline.html` and `research/timeline.json` in the same change set. Keep their entries consistent, use only supportable dates, and record the relevant artifact or commit. Rebuild generated graph data from its source registers, then update coverage notes and any affected reports. Preserve the distinction between reviewed evidence and machine-ranked leads. After a publication push, confirm the Pages build before marking the milestone published.

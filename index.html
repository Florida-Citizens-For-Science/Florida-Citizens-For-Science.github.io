Defending and promoting quality science education in the Sunshine State

Science education matters in Florida.

Florida Citizens for Science is a statewide nonprofit founded in 2006 to promote and defend quality science education. This site brings together the organization’s research and public resources; flascience.org remains the home for the organization’s broader history, issues, and blog.

Our work

Promoting sound science education

Florida Citizens for Science describes its purpose as promoting and defending quality science education in Florida. Its public work has included state science standards, instructional materials, science teaching, and efforts to weaken or distort science education. The organization’s existing site provides its history, public statements, issue archive, and extensive teacher-resource links.

Research and analysis

Research pages publish source-based analyses and supporting data. The current featured project reviews Florida’s proposed 2026 science standards against prior Florida standards and selected external sources. Findings distinguish documented text and coverage from interpretation, and state limits where the public record does not establish provenance or process.

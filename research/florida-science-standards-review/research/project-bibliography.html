Project source listing and bibliography

This bibliography gathers the sources behind the adoption and political-history findings. Citations were checked against the source publisher or an official record. Links point to public sources; DOI links use doi.org. The downloadable BibTeX file contains the same records.

Local preservation: The adopted 2008 standard, official FDOE newsletters, and enacted 2024 law are saved in the local research corpus with hashes. The 2015 investigative report reprint and accessible HTML captures of selected reporting are retained locally for research but are not redistributed here. Other copyrighted news articles are linked and summarized rather than republished. This is the first consolidated bibliography for the adoption and political-history sources; the linked corpus directories and row-level registers remain the complete source inventory for the standards comparisons. The cited reporting was checked on October 24, 2026. News reports are summarized here; source notes are not publisher abstracts.

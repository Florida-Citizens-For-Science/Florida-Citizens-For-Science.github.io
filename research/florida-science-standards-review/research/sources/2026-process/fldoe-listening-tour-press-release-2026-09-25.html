Contents relevant to the process record

The release announces the public listening tour and describes the standards rewrite as grounded in knowledge, evidence, inquiry, and reason. In a direct quotation, Commissioner Henry Mack invokes the “classical tradition,” describes nature as ordered, and says “the book of creation can be read by reason.” Its stated standards goals include distinguishing established scientific and nutrition knowledge from speculation, advocacy, and “political fashion”; respecting parents as first educators; and focusing on “the dignity of life” and “the cultivation of virtue.”

Relevance and limits

This is primary evidence of the administration’s public framing of the standards process. The classical/creation language is relevant context for the investigation’s sectarian-shading question, and the contrast between established knowledge and advocacy/political fashion is relevant to the project’s rhetoric review. The source is a press release, not a standard, workgroup draft, or record of authoring. It does not establish that religious content was inserted into the draft, that the working group adopted these framings, or that the language affected any benchmark. The draft’s wording and the administration’s public messaging must be assessed separately.

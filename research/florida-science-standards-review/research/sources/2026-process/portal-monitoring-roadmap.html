FDOE portal monitoring roadmap

Purpose: preserve a dated record of the materials on the FDOE Standards Review page, track changes, and make sure analysis covers the standards and their supporting materials as distinct parts of the review packet. Source files and full captured form text belong in the private research corpus; this page reports scope, status, and method.

What is in scope

  • Coded standards text: count parent-level standard descriptions and numbered leaf benchmarks as separate but formal text layers. The main comparison must include both layers on both sides; the narrow leaf-only count may also be shown if explicitly labeled.
  • Proposed standards: the three grade-band PDFs, K–5, grades 6–8, and grades 9–12. Do not treat the entire extracted PDF word count as standards text.
  • Supporting material: the foreword and K–12 tables PDF, including clarifications, examples, instructional notes, and other explanatory text embedded with the standards. Report these separately from numbered benchmark text.
  • Online review forms: the captured HTML contains 35 Forms anchor placements to 26 unique Microsoft Forms. The inventory records each anchor label and shared form ID. Form prompts, answer choices, and guidance may add text that does not occur in the PDFs.
  • Other portal links: keep an inventory of every link with its heading context. Classify linked resources as current standards, supporting materials, review forms, or unrelated/historical resources before deciding whether to archive them. The page also contains older standards-review records; those are tracked separately from the current science packet.

Public-comment close

As checked October 27, the FDOE page labels the period “September–October 2026” but does not state an exact online-form closing date or time. A National Center for Science Education page has been cited as reporting comments through the end of October, but that report was not retrievable for independent verification in this review. FDOE itself states only “September–October 2026” and does not confirm October 31 or a closing time. Do not treat October 31 as verified. Do not assume a precise October 31 deadline. For confirmation, contact the Bureau of Standards and Instructional Support at 850-245-0423 or use the current portal. Treat the comment window as imminent until FDOE confirms otherwise.

Preservation and reporting rules

  • Keep dated source bytes, text extracts, form captures, manifests, and checksums in the local private corpus. Do not publish full PDFs or form text without a rights review.
  • Publish a source inventory and change summary only after checking that they expose no response data, credentials, session values, or restricted material. The monitor does not enter or submit responses.
  • Label portal changes as changes to source material, not evidence of authorship, policy intent, or adoption status.
  • Update the corpus/process report, benchmark register, graph coverage, affected analyses, and both project timelines together when a research milestone is complete.

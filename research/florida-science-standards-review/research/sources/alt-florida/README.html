Self-audited match

2026 draft: Grade 6–8, SC.6.CB.1.2: “Investigate that all living organisms are composed of cells.” The benchmark is in the corpus PDF ../../fl-68.pdf, PDF page 146. Its comparison extract was made at /tmp/draft-68.txt.

FCS, April 2007: “All living organisms are composed of cells, from just one to many trillions, whose details usually are visible only through a microscope.” Source URL: https://www.flascience.org/FCSstandards.pdf; PDF p. 29 (printed p. 22). Local copy: florida-citizens-2007-standards.pdf. SHA-256: 2449acd82764b9b7c2eb501c14dcce0ed3c474d7d738ced033f29144a72cc62e.

California, 2006 framework: The same full sentence occurs at Grade 7 Life Sciences, standard 1, PDF page 112 (printed p. 104). Primary source: https://core-docs.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/1351901/Science_Framework.pdf. The official-host document is identifiable as California Department of Education’s science framework. The 2026 phrase shares only the proposition/short clause, not the sentence’s distinctive continuation. FCS says it drew on California, so matching both is a single correlated lead. This is not a verified lift into the 2026 draft.

The California 2006 PDF has a local copy at california-science-framework-2006.pdf, SHA-256 ca7f4ca9a634113f5222078bcc821b71d12f118a9bb3979ac416ef056a8fbf45; extracted text is adjacent. Whole-document comparison with normalized exact word sequences returned no 11+-token matches to the 2026 K–5, 6–8, or 9–12 drafts. The only 10-token exact sequence was “the relative positions of Earth the Moon and the Sun,” a standard description of lunar phases. Other 8–9-token matches were manually checked: Newton’s third-law phrasing, the visible-spectrum color list, fossil-fuel examples, the conventional definition of humidity, genetic information transfer, digestion-organ names, and hormone names. They are standard formulations/lists and are not counted as distinctive borrowing. Source-version anchors include California Grade 5 force benchmark 1.d and Grade 7 life-science benchmark 1; draft anchor is Florida SC.5.FM.1.2 for the force statement, plus the cell standard above.

Virginia, 2003 Standards of Learning: FCS lists Virginia as one of its source state sets. A contemporary bibliographic reference identifies “Science Standards of Learning for Virginia Public Schools” (2003) at the former VDOE PDF URL. The current Virginia public page documents later, revised standards; search-indexed results also surface Virginia 2003/2010 test materials, but the original 2003 standards PDF was not recovered in this pass. No Virginia-specific wording match is therefore claimed. The law-of-force phrasing above is common across state standards and physics teaching, so it does not establish Virginia as a 2026 source.

Older Florida comparison: The 1996 Florida grade 6–8 benchmark and extracted 2008 adopted standards do not contain this exact seven-word sequence. The later Florida benchmark SC.6.L.14.2 (2010s-era) expresses the same familiar cell-theory components (“all organisms are composed of cells,” among them); the 2026 wording can plausibly descend from Florida’s own benchmark tradition. CPALMS current record: https://www.cpalms.org/PreviewStandard/Preview/1773. This increases the ambiguity and further downgrades the California/FCS overlap.

Separate same-state predecessor wording match (not an external alternative source): The 2026 draft SC.7.SL.2.1 reads, “Explain how populations change over time as a result of births, deaths, immigration, emigration, and limiting factors” (corpus fl-68.pdf, extracted PDF page 129; local comparison extract /tmp/draft-68.txt, line 5882). The 2008 adopted Florida standards, SC.912.L.17.5, read, “Analyze how population size is determined by births, deaths, immigration, emigration, and limiting factors (biotic and abiotic) that determine carrying capacity” (PDF text page 72; local source copy florida-2008-science-standards.pdf, SHA-256 330e833b30aaaebdc6d6b0bd09c695afeee1abc3aceac5ee047cc69c2d147bdb). The normalized shared ordered phrase is 7 words: “births deaths immigration emigration and limiting factors.” This is a verified wording overlap with the immediate same-state predecessor and is reasonably classified as likely Florida lineage. Its ecological-demography list is more specific than the rejected cell-theory proposition, but the overlap alone does not establish the drafting route or external influence. The earlier phrase screen used an eight-token threshold, so it did not flag this seven-word match; this correction records it as predecessor continuity, not as a lift from an independent alternative source or evidence of inappropriate access.

The 1996 standards and GLEs were also screened: no 8+-token exact matches occurred in the grade 3–5 or K PDFs; grade 6–8 had only the conventional conservation-law wording “energy cannot be created or destroyed”; the grade 6–8 GLE had that and a short eclipse-position phrase. The current draft’s corresponding examples are recast and expand into alignment, modeling, and observation tasks. No distinctive textual lift was verified from those 1996/1998–99 sources. The separate seven-word 2008 Florida population phrase is recorded above as same-state predecessor continuity. Other short 2008 exact strings for shape-of-container, solar-system objects, and matter/energy movement are conventional scientific wording.

The FCS PDF’s 7–8-token overlaps with the 2026 PDFs were limited to short textbook/commonplace wording: the cell-theory proposition; a digestive-organ sequence; conservation-of-energy language; and a phrase about solar-system objects. There were no 9-token exact matches. All were rejected as verified borrowing. Matching was case- and punctuation-insensitive after PDF text extraction; all candidate matches were manually checked in context.

The named pre-adoption draft therefore remains a high-priority missing primary version, not a reviewed source.
  • The 2007 FDOE portal’s linked framers presentations and several third-party standards PDFs returned cache misses in the browsing connector. They have not been represented as searched for exact wording.

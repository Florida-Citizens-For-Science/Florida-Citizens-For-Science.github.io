Deep crosswalk pass: high-school forces, motion, and energy

Review date: October 22, 2026
Scope: 23 previously unresolved high-school FM benchmarks, compared with Florida 2008, the surviving 1996 high-school standards, and the available 1998/99 GLE text.

Results

This pass added 21 concept-level predecessor mappings (8 high-confidence and 13 medium-confidence). Two fluid-mechanics benchmarks remained unresolved at this review checkpoint; the current register has one unresolved fluid-mechanics benchmark, SC.912.FM.3.4. Each mapping is anchored to the cited 2008 benchmark in the register, with a note describing the degree of fit and current-draft detail not stated in the predecessor.

Continuity is strongest for Newton’s laws, kinematic descriptions, scalar/vector quantities and reference frames, work, energy conservation, potential-energy diagrams, electrostatic fields, and ideal-gas models. Florida 2008 SC.912.P.12.1–.4 covers vectors, motion functions, Newton’s laws, and gravitational-force relationships; SC.912.P.10.2–.3 covers conservation, work, and power; SC.912.P.10.6 covers potential-energy diagrams; and SC.912.P.10.13 covers electrostatic fields and forces. Several draft tasks add named applications, such as centripetal motion, free-body diagrams, mechanical-energy conditions, or interpreting force from potential-energy graphs.

At that review checkpoint, the two unresolved rows concerned buoyancy/fluid motion and fluid-flow analysis using pressure differences and conservation principles. The current register still leaves SC.912.FM.3.4 unresolved; the buoyancy row now has a concept-match classification. No specific predecessor for SC.912.FM.3.4 was located in the reviewed 2008/1996/GLE corpus. This is a source-based unresolved result, not proof the concept is new or absent from instruction.

Method and limits

All 23 previously unresolved FM leaves were checked against the full 2008 force/motion/energy text and relevant surviving 1996 material; GLEs only cover K–8 and therefore cannot supply a high-school predecessor. Broad topical similarity alone was not treated as a match. No divergence or non-divergent finding was inferred from retrieval absence. See the register for exact rows and source line ranges.

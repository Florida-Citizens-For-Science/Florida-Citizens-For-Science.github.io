Deep crosswalk pass: high-school nutrition foundations and food systems

Review date: October 22, 2026
Scope: All 19 initially unresolved grade 9–12 NT benchmarks in the register, screened against the locally preserved Florida 2008 standards, 1996 standards, and surviving 1998/99 GLEs.

Results

This pass found three foundational concept matches to 2008, five medium-confidence links to nutrient concepts or nature-of-science operations, ten benchmark-level divergence findings, and one unresolved comparison. The 2008 standards contain macromolecule structures/functions, carbohydrate metabolism, digestion, cellular energy, and general scientific research/evidence standards. The surviving GLE includes a much earlier expectation about food groups and nutritional value. None of those is equivalent to the draft’s modern food-processing, label-literacy, or processed-food health claims.

Still unresolved

SC.912.NT.4.1 asks how water contributes to cellular hydration, circulation, and temperature regulation. The 2008 corpus has related water, circulation, and body-system content, but the focused screen did not establish a sufficiently specific predecessor for this integrated hydration task. It remains unresolved rather than being called either new or equivalent.

Earlier Florida material and limits

The surviving 1998/99 K–2 GLE (SC.B.1.1.5) asks about food’s nutritional value and classifies food groups; grades 3–5 SC.D.2.2.1 concerns reducing natural-resource use. These support broad elementary concepts, not high-school food-processing frameworks, marketing, or dietary-health outcomes. No high-school GLE text is present in the corpus. A missing topic in the reviewed standards does not prove absence from every intended lesson or other instructional material. Content-level differences do not identify a source, author, drafting route, or access event.

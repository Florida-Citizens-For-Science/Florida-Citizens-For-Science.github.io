Deep crosswalk pass: K–8 nutrition and food systems

Review date: October 22, 2026
Scope: Previously unresolved K–8 NT benchmarks dealing with human food, nutrition, and related systems.

Results

This direct review added 19 concept-level mappings: 2 high-confidence and 17 medium-confidence. They are recorded in the benchmark register with source IDs, local line locators, and row-specific notes. The strongest continuity is for food/water as basic needs, energy from food, sensory observation, stomach/intestine identification, digestion, absorption, cell transport, human body-system interaction, waste removal, and plant-to-animal food-energy pathways. Several concepts move from grade 4–6 or even high-school predecessor statements into K–5 draft benchmarks.

The GLEs support selected food-energy and general nutrient concepts, but the surviving files do not establish a complete health/nutrition strand. Many current NT details remain unmatched: food-group taxonomy, nutrient composition/macronutrients, nutritional effects of preparation and processing, hunger/satiety, food-waste reduction, label analysis, food cost/social factors, and lifespan-specific nutrition patterns. These are retained as unresolved where the predecessor discussion was too broad. They are not automatically classified as omissions or new content.

Method and limits

Reviewed each mapped row against relevant K–8 2008 life-science standards and GLEs, with later-grade body-system standards cited only as conceptual predecessors and the grade displacement stated. The 1996 text was also checked; its surviving lower-grade corpus emphasizes basic needs and food energy, not a detailed human nutrition curriculum. This pass does not adjudicate every nutrition-related instructional material or health standard outside the research corpus.

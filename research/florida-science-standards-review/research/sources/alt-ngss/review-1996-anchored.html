Source-first task-equivalence review: anchored 1996 Florida benchmarks

Scope and method

This appendix reviews all 34 entries in sources/alt-ngss/legacy-reverse-screen.csv where source_family=1996 Florida and direct_anchor_in_register=yes, including rows that already had prose dispositions in the register screen. I checked each source benchmark in the preserved 1996 extract (and its PDF page), including numbered expectations visible after a section heading. Register anchors and similarity candidates were used to locate draft sections, not as equivalence verdicts. I then checked the relevant 2026 benchmark wording and, where the classification depends on it, its clarification, example/task, or adjacent benchmarks in the same strand. Source and draft line locators below refer to the preserved .txt extracts; the source PDF page is also given.

R means the central source task/concept is retained or relocated in the current draft. P means some material is present, while at least one meaningful source task/concept is narrowed, implicit, or absent from the reviewed candidate set. O means a possible corpus-bounded omission of a source-specific concept/task; it is not evidence of classroom absence or provenance. U means the comparison remains unresolved. A match, including an R classification, does not establish drafting access, borrowing, or provenance.

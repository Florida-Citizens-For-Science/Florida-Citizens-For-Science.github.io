Scope and result

This appendix reviews all 172 SC.912.* entries marked direct_anchor_in_register=yes in sources/alt-ngss/2008-reverse-screen.csv. Each complete 2008 benchmark was checked in the preserved PDF and extracted text against the 2026 draft benchmark text, then against relevant neighboring draft benchmarks, clarifications, examples, and student tasks. Register links and similarity ranks were used only to locate candidates.

A conceptual match does not establish drafting access, copying, or provenance. O means a corpus-bounded candidate for a source-specific task/concept not located in the preserved 2026 corpus; it is not proof of classroom absence or provenance. U marks a comparison that remains indeterminate after review.

Method and locators

Source locators are line numbers in sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.txt; I verified each ID and its complete benchmark in that extract and the preserved PDF. Draft locators point to sources/2026/fl-k5.txt, sources/2026/fl-68.txt, or sources/2026/fl-912.txt. The registered target links shown in each row are selected as source-specific locators; their actual draft lead text is printed so the link can be judged. A nearby benchmark is added when an anchor list misses the relevant current strand. Text after the benchmark anchor, including clarifications, examples, and tasks, and nearby same-strand benchmarks was checked where relevant.

Common practice supports checked where applicable: inquiry/evidence/modeling framing in sources/2026/fl-foreword.txt:78-84,119-146; energy/matter/force modeling and lab practices in sources/2026/fl-tables.txt:3047-3223; Earth/space supports in fl-tables.txt:4236-4419; life-science supports in fl-tables.txt:4902-5082. General references to evidence or investigation do not by themselves preserve each standalone 2008 competency.

Section-leading rows in the 2008 extract can carry a body-of-knowledge heading in CSV source_text; the short task label below is normalized to benchmark wording adjacent to the cited source ID. The extract and PDF remain authoritative.

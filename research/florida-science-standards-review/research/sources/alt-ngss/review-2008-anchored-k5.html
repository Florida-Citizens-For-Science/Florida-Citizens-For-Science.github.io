Source-first task-equivalence review: anchored 2008 Florida K–5 benchmarks

Scope and method

This appendix covers every row in 2008-reverse-screen.csv where direct_anchor_in_register=yes and source_id is in grades K–5 (84 IDs). For each row, I read the complete 2008 benchmark from the preserved text extract and compared its student operation and concept with the directly linked 2026 benchmark(s), then checked relevant neighboring 2026 benchmark wording, clarifications, examples, and tasks in the K–5, 6–8, and 9–12 extracts. The PDF page index is the stored physical page index in the reverse-screen register; the extract line is a physical line number. The 2026 line locator points to the benchmark heading in the named extracted file. Where a judgment cites adjacent concepts, those are named in the finding so they can be checked in context. Similarity scores and direct code anchors are locators only, not evidence of equivalence.

Classes: R = retained/relocated; P = partial; O = possible omission of the source-specific task/concept; U = unresolved. R requires the core task/concept to be present in one or more draft outcomes, even if relocated. P means a related concept remains but a meaningful part of the source task or proposition does not. O is a corpus-bounded candidate: the specific task/concept was not located in the preserved draft corpus. It is not proof of classroom absence or provenance. U means the corpus does not permit a defensible decision.

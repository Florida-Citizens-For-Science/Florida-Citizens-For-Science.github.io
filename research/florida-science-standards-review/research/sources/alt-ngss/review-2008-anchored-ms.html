Source-first task-equivalence audit: anchored 2008 Florida middle-school benchmarks

Scope and result

This appendix audits every 2008 Florida benchmark row in sources/alt-ngss/2008-reverse-screen.csv whose direct_anchor_in_register is yes and whose source grade is 6, 7, or 8. The bounded filter returns 72 unique IDs. Each appears once in the row table below. Draft code anchors and similarity-ranked candidates were used only to find passages; neither was treated as an equivalence verdict.

Class Count
R — retained/relocated 31
P — partial 40
O — possible omission of a source-specific task/concept 1
U — unresolved 0
Total 72

O is a corpus-bounded candidate only. It means the source-specific task/concept was not located after checking relevant current corpus text; it is not proof of classroom absence, nor evidence about provenance or why a standard changed. P means meaningful overlap exists but a named element, scope, or task demand is missing or altered. R includes clear grade/strand relocation and reasonable distribution across benchmarks. U is reserved for a case the current corpus does not permit us to assess.

Method and evidence boundary

I filtered the CSV to the 72 exact rows above, then read each full source benchmark passage from sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.txt at the cited line and checked its PDF page in sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.pdf (the page numbers shown are 1-based, corresponding to the CSV source_pdf_page_index). For each row I opened the linked 2026 benchmark text in sources/2026/fl-k5.txt, fl-68.txt, or fl-912.txt, then checked nearby related benchmark text and, where applicable, the clarification, example, student task, common framework, table, or practice passages in those extracts. The table gives at least one 2026 line locator per row; grouped ranges refer only to the stated related benchmarks. The marked draft lines are locators, not the whole evidence record. The adjudication asks whether the full source’s expected student action and concept survive—not whether a code, phrase, or topic is still present.

Extract line references are stable locators in the supplied text files; they do not replace the PDFs as the authoritative source. No material outside this corpus was used to infer classroom instruction, local implementation, or drafting provenance. The audit is a corpus review, not an experimental assessment of instruction.

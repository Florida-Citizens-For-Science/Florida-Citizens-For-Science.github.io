Source-first task-equivalence review: unanchored 1998/99 Florida GLEs, grades 6–8

Scope and method

This appendix reviews the 15 entries in sources/alt-ngss/legacy-reverse-screen.csv where source_family=1998/99 GLE, source_grade_band=68, and direct_anchor_in_register=no. Each complete source benchmark and its sixth-, seventh-, and eighth-grade expectations were checked in sources/1998-1999/florida-gle-science-68.txt against the preserved PDF page. Candidate ranks were used only as locators. I checked 2026 benchmarks across grades, including relevant examples, clarifications, and neighboring text; support pages were also consulted where cited. The current draft’s broad modeling, investigation, or evidence language does not by itself preserve each source-specific content expectation.

R means the source’s central task/concept is retained or relocated. P means some source content is present, but a meaningful task or concept is narrowed, implicit, or not located in the relevant benchmarks. O means a possible corpus-bounded omission of source-specific content, not proof of classroom absence or provenance. U means the comparison remains unresolved after review. Concept similarity does not establish drafting access, copying, or provenance.

The source locators below give the extract line and PDF page. Draft citations give the benchmark ID and its line in the current sources/2026/fl-k5.txt, fl-68.txt, or fl-912.txt extract. Where useful, neighboring benchmark clarifications/tasks were read beyond the cited benchmark heading. Support-page context checked includes the 6–8 energy/matter/force modeling and laboratory practice sections (sources/2026/fl-tables.txt:1699–1792), 6–8 life-science modeling and laboratory practices (fl-tables.txt:2713–2806), and corresponding 9–12 energy and life modeling sections (fl-tables.txt:3047–3220, 4902–5082). These supports aid performance of tasks but do not add a missing disciplinary concept to the benchmark text.

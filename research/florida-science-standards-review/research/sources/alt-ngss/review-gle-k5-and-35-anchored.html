Source-first task-equivalence review: anchored 1998/99 GLE kindergarten and grades 3–5

Scope and method

This appendix reviews all 52 directly anchored 1998/99 GLE rows in legacy-reverse-screen.csv for kindergarten and grades 3–5: 19 kindergarten rows and 33 grades 3–5 rows. The table combines the 46 previously reviewed register rows with six additional rows that carried prior human-reviewed dispositions. Each complete source benchmark and grade-level-expectations passage was read from its preserved GLE extract and checked against the linked 2026 benchmark(s) and relevant neighboring 2026 benchmark text, clarifications, examples, and student tasks in the K–5, 6–8, and 9–12 extracts. Source line numbers and PDF page numbers follow the register. Draft line numbers use LF-only physical lines (split on \n); citations point to the occurrence of each benchmark ID in the named draft extract. Similarity scores and direct code anchors are locators, not equivalence findings.

Classes: R = retained/relocated; P = partial; O = possible omission of the source-specific task/concept; U = unresolved. R means the core task/concept appears in one or more draft outcomes, even if reorganized or moved across grades. P means a related concept remains but an important part of the source passage is not explicit. O is a corpus-bounded candidate only; it does not prove classroom absence or provenance. U means the available corpus does not support a defensible decision.

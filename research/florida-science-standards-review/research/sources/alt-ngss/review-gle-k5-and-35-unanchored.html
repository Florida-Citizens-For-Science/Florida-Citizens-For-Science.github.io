Source-first task-equivalence review: unanchored 1998/99 GLE K and grades 3–5

Scope and method

This appendix covers all 30 1998/99 GLE rows in legacy-reverse-screen.csv with direct_anchor_in_register=no and source_grade_band K or 3–5: 15 kindergarten IDs and 15 grades 3–5 IDs. For each row, the complete source benchmark and its associated grade-level expectations were read in the preserved PDF/extract. The source-specific operation and concept were then compared against the full K–12 2026 draft extracts, including related standards across grades, clarifications, examples, and student tasks. The first-ranked reverse-screen candidate was treated only as a search lead; a locator is not an equivalence judgment. Source PDF pages and extract lines follow the register. Draft line numbers use LF-only physical lines (split on \n) and point to the benchmark-heading occurrence in the named text extract.

Classes: R = retained/relocated; P = partial; O = possible omission of a source-specific task/concept; U = unresolved. R means the core task/concept appears somewhere in the preserved draft. P means related content appears but a meaningful part of the source is not explicit. O is a corpus-bounded candidate, not proof of classroom absence or provenance. U means the corpus does not support a defensible decision.

Method and interpretation

I compared each draft row with the full preserved Florida 2008 standards and Florida 1996 grade 9–12 text, the complete surviving 1998/99 GLE volumes (K, 3–5, 6–8), and the locally preserved external standards corpus: NGSS 2017 topic-arranged standards, NGSS 2015 middle-school life-science evidence statements, NGSS Appendix E (2013), Massachusetts 2006 and 2016, South Carolina 2014, California 2006, and Florida Citizens/FCS 2007. Full-text term and variant searches were used to find candidate passages; all positive candidates were read in context. The row descriptions below incorporate the earlier source-by-source A-strand audit at deep-crosswalk-hs-animals-residual.md. “None located” means no sufficiently specific passage was found in this preserved corpus, not that a concept is novel or absent from instruction. The GLE collection contains no high-school volume, so lower-grade matches are context only. External parallels are comparators and do not establish Florida drafting access or provenance.

For compactness, the evidence keys cite exact physical LF line locators in the local extracted text files. F08 is Florida 2008; F96 is Florida 1996. Draft locators below point to sources/2026/fl-912.txt lines containing the benchmark ID; register PDF pages are also shown where available.

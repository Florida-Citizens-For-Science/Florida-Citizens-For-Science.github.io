Review of unresolved high-school benchmarks: EN, M, FM, SP, CB, P, and NT

Review date: October 22, 2026
Scope: 32 grade 9–12 benchmark IDs still marked unresolved in the 2026 register, excluding the A, HB, ASL, FG, and MO strands. The review compared the complete benchmark/clarification passages in sources/2026/fl-912.txt with the full Florida 2008 and surviving 1996 standards, all preserved 1998/99 GLE files, the full NGSS text, Massachusetts 2006/2016, and South Carolina 2014 corpus texts.
Register and graph: Not edited in this review.

Method and interpretation

I read each draft benchmark with its clarification text, then searched complete local source texts for both exact terms and underlying concepts. I checked the existing matter, plant, nutrition, and other high-school crosswalk reviews for previously verified source passages. Semantic retrieval was used as a discovery aid; classifications below rely on source passages and their stated scope, not rank alone. Locators use physical LF line numbers in the checked-in text files; printed page references are supplied where available.

The surviving 1998/99 Florida GLE files cover K–8, not grades 9–12. A GLE reference below therefore means an earlier cross-grade concept, never a high-school GLE. External standards show that a concept or task appears in another jurisdiction; they do not establish that Florida’s workgroup used that source. “Remain unresolved” means the reviewed Florida predecessor corpus does not establish a sufficiently specific match or divergence. Lack of a located passage is not treated as proof of novelty, instructional omission, or intent.

Corpus-wide observations and source-text checks

  • The directly useful Florida passages are concentrated in 2008 matter/energy (SC.912.P.8.1–.13, lines 2948–2995; SC.912.P.10.1–.8, lines 3014–3040), cell and endocrine/nervous-system benchmarks (SC.912.L.14.2–.5, .14.21–.31, lines 3184–3200 and 3252–3294), Earth materials (SC.912.E.6.1–.2, lines 2850–2858; grade-4 mineral/rock benchmarks at 1480–1502), and elementary object/force content (SC.5.E.5.3, lines 1727–1729; grade 6–8 buoyancy GLE lines 463–467). The 1996 text supplies broad phase-change, thermodynamics, signaling, and cell communication concepts; it does not contain the named modern tasks reviewed here.
  • The external texts yield discrete content analogues, not a coherent source set for the 2026 passage sequence: South Carolina 2014 covers straight-chain alkanes and structural isomers (H.C.3A.2/.5, lines 3462–3474), molarity/solubility (H.C.5A.2/.3, lines 3528–3537), and asteroid/comet impacts (8.E.6A.3, lines 2839–2846). Massachusetts 2006 covers buffers (8.3, lines 3424–3428), molarity (7.2, lines 3400–3404), VSEPR, and chemistry topics. Massachusetts 2016 adds explicit dipole interactions, redox devices, and exclusions/boundaries for equilibrium constants and Gibbs-free-energy calculations. These are comparisons of available standards text; no distinctive wording lift was established in this set.
  • Full-text searches across all preserved 1996, 1998/99, 2008, NGSS, Massachusetts, and South Carolina source texts did not locate Florida predecessors for grid dispatch/peak shaving, intracellular signaling cascades, plant hormones, medicinal plants, near-Earth risk analysis, or the listed detailed acid/base and electrochemical procedures. The identified broad parallels are recorded row by row; those negative searches do not prove those topics were absent from all Florida instructional materials.
  • Source-extraction checks found the 2026 PDF-derived text uses page headers inside passages and typographic ligature characters (for example, Clariﬁcation and aﬀect). The IDs and benchmark text remain readable and searchable; page and line locators in this review refer to the extracted .txt, not PDF page order. In the register, text_extract is a source-path pointer rather than extracted benchmark wording; the wording is stored in benchmark_text_register. This is a schema-label ambiguity, not evidence of missing benchmark content. No benchmark omission or parser truncation was confirmed for the 32 reviewed IDs.

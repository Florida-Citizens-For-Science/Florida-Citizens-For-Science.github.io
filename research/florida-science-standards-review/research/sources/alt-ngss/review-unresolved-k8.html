Source review: unresolved K–8 draft rows

Review date: 2026-10-22. This review began with 14 K–8 rows marked unresolved vs research corpus after the earlier K–8 nutrition, biology, and residual crosswalks. A later component review updated six rows with specific partial-predecessor links; eight of the original rows remain unresolved. The five ranked source candidates were treated as search leads and checked against the underlying source passages and related standards. These are review dispositions; rows remain unresolved where the corpus supports only component concepts or no specific predecessor.

SC.2.NT.1.4 — food from production to consumption Florida GLE SC.B.1.1.5 describes food as human energy and food groups/nutritional value (K GLE text, lines 179–195); 2008 SC.4.L.17.2–.3 follows energy from plants/animals through food chains (2008 text, lines 1640–1645). Remain unresolved; no supply-chain predecessor located. The ranked candidates were unrelated nature-of-science benchmarks. Food needs, nutrition, and trophic energy flow do not establish a predecessor for tracing food from where it is grown to where it is eaten.
SC.7.NT.1.5 — food types and energy regulation GLE SC.B.1.1.5 names food groups, nutritional value, and food energy; NGSS 5-PS3-1 connects food materials with body repair/growth and energy (sources/1998-1999/florida-gle-science-k.txt:177–189; sources/alt-ngss/ngss-all-topic-standards.txt:1630–1631). Medium-confidence partial match; integrated into register. Food categories and energy/body-needs components have predecessors. Hunger, satiety, and regulation by food type remain absent from those passages.
SC.7.NT.1.6–.7, SC.8.NT.1.2–.4, .1.6–.7 — hydration, processed foods, lifespan health, social/environmental factors, food labels, cost/preparation (7 benchmarks) The GLE and Florida 2008 cover food groups/value/energy, body systems, homeostasis, and water properties in separate benchmarks (GLE SC.B.1.1.5, lines 177–189; 2008 SC.6.L.14.5, lines 2114–2118; SC.912.L.18.12, lines 3680–3683; SC.4.L.17.2–.3, lines 1640–1645). Remain unresolved; partial or adjacent components only. The reviewed predecessors do not specify hydration physiology or beverage effects, processed-food health claims, nutrition patterns over the lifespan, social/environmental determinants, food-label comparison, or the integrated preparation/cost/composition tasks. The deeper source audit is deep-crosswalk-k8-nutrition.md, which also records the matched nutrition concepts and its scope limits.
SC.8.MO.1.2 — microorganisms’ influence on environmental and human systems Florida 2008 SC.6.L.14.6 names infectious-agent groups (sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.txt:2119–2120); GLE SC.D.1.3.4 describes bacteria/fungi effects on soil/plant growth (sources/1998-1999/florida-gle-science-68.txt:629–642); NGSS LS2.A covers decomposition/material cycling (sources/alt-ngss/ngss-all-topic-standards.txt:1654–1657). Medium-confidence partial match; integrated into register. Separate human-health and environmental roles are explicit; the draft’s integrated cross-system microorganism analysis is additional. NGSS is a comparator, not evidence of drafting access. See deep-crosswalk-middle-residual.md.
SC.6.ASL.1.1 and SC.6.ASL.1.2 — integrated ecosystem/organism applications Florida 2008 SC.7.L.17.1–.3, SC.6.L.15.1, SC.6.L.14.4, and SC.7.L.15.2–.3 (sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.txt:2110–2131,2299–2304,2341–2352); GLE SC.G.2.3.4 (sources/1998-1999/florida-gle-science-68.txt:1192–1202). The draft’s data-based problem and synthesis tasks are additional.
SC.8.ASL.1.1 and SC.8.ASL.1.2 — integrated ecosystem change/stability applications Florida 2008 SC.7.L.17.1, SC.7.L.15.2–.3, SC.7.L.17.3, and SC.912.L.17.16; NGSS MS-LS2-1, MS-LS2-3–5, and HS-LS2-6 (sources/2008/florida-2008-science-standards.txt:2299–2304,2343–2352,3610–3612; sources/alt-ngss/ngss-all-topic-standards.txt:2660–2668,2810–2812,4737–4740).

The graph links Florida science benchmarks to earlier Florida standards, national and state frameworks, and reviewed comparison records. Search for a topic or benchmark, focus on its recorded neighborhood, and open its source and evidence notes in a detail window.

How to read the graph

Solid connections represent recorded comparison assertions. Dashed amber connections mark standards files named in locators for composite crosswalk items that have no single source assignment; these citations add context but do not claim benchmark equivalence. Dashed purple connections in a focused view are machine-ranked similarity leads and remain unreviewed. They are discovery aids, not evidence of copying, authorship, or access. Each item detail identifies the relationship label, evidence note, and confidence where recorded.

The graph contains benchmark items from the 1996 Florida standards, 1998/99 grade-level elaborations, adopted 2008 Florida standards, proposed 2026 draft, NGSS, Indiana 2000, and the 2007 Florida Citizens for Science proposal, plus document-level process and framework records. Source files remain at their public hosts unless redistribution has been checked.

Concept labels are analytical groupings, not FDOE topic titles. In the October 26 packet, one high-school marine-life benchmark uses the phrase “evolutionary processes,” and its clarification includes common ancestry; the packet has no separately titled evolution standard or topic. Read the wording and placement analysis.

The Franklin Standards (2024) now appear as a cited source document with five public paraphrase units and five reviewed topic-comparison links. Its complete 133-page text is held and page-indexed in the private corpus, not reproduced in the downloadable public graph. The links record content comparisons, not drafting-source evidence. Read the source analysis.

Coverage limits: the historical graph represents all 1,512 first-round leaf benchmarks; the current graph represents 1,573 leaf benchmarks and 339 parent descriptions. The 498 Florida 2008 benchmark IDs are represented. Coverage for older and external sources varies. The graph also contains 3,354 machine-ranked leads. Some derived crosswalk items cite multiple source files without a single source-version assignment. Read the source-coverage notes and October 26 ID and sentence crosswalk before drawing conclusions from the map.

Curriculum-material inventory is a focused first pass: 19 OpenSciEd matches have been verified. Other benchmarks remain not inventoried; a missing entry is not evidence that no material exists.

Controls and accessibility

The app keeps its canvas within the viewport. The benchmark network opens with the 2026 draft selected, and its nodes are spaced within grade/course bands. Use source and grade filters or search for an exact benchmark code; the results list offers an individually labeled route to every encoded item. Hover or focus a map point for its code, then select it for details. Compare older and external versions with the source checkboxes. The source map has a curated layout and an option to group records by source family. Drag the map or focus the canvas and use arrow keys to pan; use the wheel or zoom controls for up to 400% magnification. The Center button preserves filters and zoom; Reset view clears them. Item details appear in a dialog, and source links open separately.

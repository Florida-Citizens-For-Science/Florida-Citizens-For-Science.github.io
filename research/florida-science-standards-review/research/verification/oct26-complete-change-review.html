What changed in the October 26 science standards draft

A full code-and-wording reconciliation of the 1,573 numbered leaf benchmarks in FDOE’s October 26 second-round PDFs against the first-round benchmark register, plus a check of FDOE’s Table of Edits for K-12 Science Standards.

Scope: “Complete” here means every leaf benchmark ID and its benchmark sentence, checked by ID and normalized exact text. The published row-level files make that comparison reproducible. The table of edits also describes selected parent-standard, clarification, example, task, and supplemental-table edits; this review does not claim an exhaustive word-for-word change log for those supporting layers. Both rounds’ full PDFs and text extractions are retained in the local research corpus.

What FDOE’s table documents and what it leaves out

FDOE’s PDF is a feedback-to-response summary organized by grade band and supplemental tables. It gives reasons for selected edits (for example, adding amplitude, adding approximate Earth and universe ages to supporting material, and adding content for specialized high-school courses). It does not provide before-and-after text for each cited change or present itself as a code-by-code, sentence-by-sentence version history. Our downloadable Table of Edits provides the exact first-round and October 26 wording for all 110 changed/new current-code rows, source locators, exact reindex matches, and a flag showing whether FDOE lists that code or its parent. The check found 18 changed/new leaf IDs for which neither the exact code nor an encompassing standard code appears in FDOE’s table. This confirms that FDOE’s PDF is not an exhaustive list of benchmark-level changes.

How to use the FDOE table alongside this review

  • Use the FDOE table for the Department’s stated feedback categories, responses, and brief rationales.
  • View and search our Table of Edits in the browser or download its CSV for the 110 changed/new current-code rows, old and new wording, locators, reindex matches, and whether FDOE’s table identifies each code or parent.
  • Use the full crosswalk for every leaf benchmark ID, exact October 26 sentence, first-round sentence at the same code, and exact sentence matches elsewhere.
  • Use the reindex map to follow sentences moved to different codes.
  • Use the row-level predecessor review for bounded content-level assessments of changed and new/reindexed rows. These are selected-source judgments, not exhaustive equivalence or provenance findings.

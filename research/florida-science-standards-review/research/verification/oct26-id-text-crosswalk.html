October 26 ID and benchmark wording crosswalk

A coded-text comparison of the first-round benchmark register and FDOE’s second-round PDFs dated October 26. It aligns codes and benchmark sentences; it does not compare full guidance, examples, student tasks, or conceptual equivalence.

Correction to the earlier inventory: the prior regex required a word boundary after the final digit and missed SC.8.NT.1.5, which the PDF text extraction concatenates with “Evaluate.” Visual inspection confirms this is a benchmark on grades 6–8 PDF p. 230. Restoring it changes the current total and predecessor-set reconciliation by one.

These are code/text placements established by string comparison; they do not establish why the sequence was reorganized. “No exact sentence match” does not mean the concept is new.

The 30 same-ID wording changes and 55 new IDs without an exact old sentence remain for conceptual and task-level comparison with the 2008 standards. That is 85 residual records; this file does not label them as new concepts, retained outcomes, or omissions.

Method and limits

  • First-round coded wording and predecessor anchors come from the project’s first-round benchmark register.
  • October 26 coded sentences were extracted from the three preserved FDOE grade-band PDFs. The comparison applies Unicode NFKC and whitespace normalization; it does not use semantic similarity to call statements equivalent.
  • The joined-code case (SC.8.NT.1.5) was checked visually on PDF p. 230 and explicitly restored. PDF pages come from page-form-feed position in the preserved pdftotext -layout extraction.
  • The main CSV retains current sentence, prior sentence at the same ID, exact text matches at another ID, first-round predecessor anchors, and source page/hash data.

Row classifications

  • shared_id_identical_text: same ID and same coded sentence after normalization. The prior 2008 comparison can carry forward for that sentence, although current parent descriptions, clarifications, examples, and tasks are not adjudicated here.
  • shared_id_wording_changed: same ID, revised sentence, and no exact reindex of the old sentence was located. The predecessor comparison must be revisited.
  • shared_id_sentence_reindexed: the old sentence appears verbatim under another current code. The mapping file lists source and destination IDs.
  • new_id_exact_sentence_reindexed: a new code carries an exact first-round sentence.
  • new_id_no_exact_old_sentence: the new sentence has no exact match in the old register. A conceptual or task-level predecessor may still exist.

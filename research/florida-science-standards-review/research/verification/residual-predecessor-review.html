How to interpret the categories

  • Concept continuity: the cited predecessor text addresses substantially the same concept or benchmark task, allowing for wording or code changes.
  • Partial match: an older benchmark addresses a related concept but does not state all of the current task, level of specificity, or application.
  • Candidate-specificity gap: the cited 2008 text supports the broader topic, while the particular application or task named in the current sentence was not located in the cited candidate text. This does not mean the broader topic was absent elsewhere.
  • Unresolved: the available first-round anchor or movement record did not support a specific predecessor judgment. It is not a gap finding.

Examples and limits

Several new-code sentences have clear earlier counterparts despite lacking exact first-round sentence matches. For example, SC.912.HB.6.8 (“Explain the physiology of skeletal muscle”) matches 2008 SC.912.L.14.19; the major-muscle identification task in SC.912.HB.6.3 is supported by 2008 SC.912.L.14.20. Likewise, the radiometric-dating application in SC.912.PE.7.6 has a clear predecessor in SC.7.E.6.3, which names radioactive dating as a method for determining Earth’s age. These are continuity findings even though the sentences/codes differ.

Other current sentences add a task or specificity that the cited older text does not state as directly. A changed sentence about avoiding wasted electricity, for example, shifts toward a behavior-level task compared with the first-round wording; the bounded review did not establish a specific 2008 predecessor anchor for that row, so its 2008 relationship remains unresolved. Such comparisons support only the stated row-level assessment, not a claim that a subject was never taught or appeared nowhere else in the 2008 standards.

The 2008 candidates were selected from the existing first-round predecessor anchors and a targeted reverse-screen register. This is not a complete semantic search of every sentence in every historical Florida source. Page numbers refer to preserved PDF pagination where recorded; extract-line locators identify the retained text used for comparison. The source register itself marks the reverse screen as targeted. Similarity rankings were used only to find candidates, not as evidence of equivalence.

These predecessor relationships show textual or task-level continuity and difference. They do not show that the 2008 text caused the 2026 wording, that a candidate-specificity gap is deliberate, or that the full standards package has no equivalent language in clarifications, examples, or other supplemental materials. This review is limited to the coded benchmark statements and listed predecessor sources.

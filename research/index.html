Featured: Florida science standards review

A full-draft review of Florida’s proposed 2026 science standards, comparing them with prior Florida standards and selected external references. The analysis covers wording and possible source relationships, grade placement, omissions, evolution and climate content, process records, metadata, learning order, and classroom burden. A finding of no divergence is bounded by the named corpus; unresolved provenance is marked for further investigation.

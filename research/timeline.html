Florida science standards review timeline

This chronology records public, checkable milestones in the research artifacts. Dates come from report cutoffs, source checks, and repository history. The sequence should not be read as a claim that work began on October 22; it does not capture off-repository research activity.

  1. October 22, 2026

    Research snapshot and public-record check

    The main comparison and corpus/process reports identify October 22 as the research cutoff. They record a check of the FDOE public-review portal and SHA-256 comparison showing the five preserved draft PDFs matched the linked portal copies that day.

    Corpus, process, and metadata report
  2. October 23, 2026

    Review hub and core analyses published

    Repository history records the standards-review hub and successive additions on 2008 and external-source comparisons, omission review, evolution and sectarian framing, provenance, human and generative-AI disclosures, public summaries, learning order, and teacher/student burden methods.

    Open the standards-review hub
  3. October 24, 2026

    Interactive graph and coverage reporting

    The graph explorer was added with searchable draft and source items, direct links to related source records, item sharing, and explicit notes on uneven corpus coverage. The viewer now refocuses to a selected node and its connected neighborhood at a chosen depth.

    Open the interactive graph
  4. October 24, 2026

    Organization research section prepared

    The new organization site structure provides a public Research landing page and places the standards review beneath it. The organization repository reports no push permission for the authenticated account at the time of preparation, so this milestone is marked prepared rather than published.

    Research landing page
